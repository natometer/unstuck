How it works.

UNstuck is built on four bodies of research, one per category. Each technique is short and evidence-based, and designed to move something without requiring you to feel ready first.

Calm

Anxiety and overwhelm are physiological states before they are cognitive ones. Breathing, grounding, and shaking work by interrupting the body's threat response, not by reframing the thoughts.

Slow breathing · Zaccaro et al. (2018). Frontiers in Human Neuroscience.  ·  DBT grounding · Linehan (1993).  ·  Somatic Experiencing · Levine (1997).
Action

Motivation follows action, not the other way around. Specifying the smallest possible concrete step, rather than just intending to act, dramatically increases follow-through, even for tasks you are dreading.

Implementation intentions · Gollwitzer (1999). American Psychologist, 54(7).  ·  Temporal Motivation Theory · Steel (2007). Psychological Bulletin, 133(1).
Regulation

Naming an emotion reduces its charge without suppressing it. Touch activates the caregiving system. Social connection, even just anticipated, regulates the neural threat response.

Affect labeling · Lieberman et al. (2007). Psychological Science, 18(5).  ·  Self-compassion · Neff (2003). Self and Identity, 2(2).  ·  Social regulation · Coan et al. (2006). Psychological Science, 17(12).
Focus

Vividly imagining a future self changes how present decisions feel. Describing a solved state accesses knowledge you already have. Recalling recent competent actions counters the negativity bias that dominates when stuck.

Future self-continuity · Hershfield (2011). Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences, 1235.  ·  Solution-focused brief therapy · de Shazer (1988).  ·  Positive psychology · Seligman et al. (2005). American Psychologist, 60(5).
